# Problem Statement

Many students do not have a clear, measurable view of their placement preparation. They may perform well academically but remain weak in coding, aptitude, projects, communication or interviews. This project builds a Java-based decision-support system that predicts a student's placement-readiness category and recommends specific improvements.

## Scope

The prototype accepts a student's preparation indicators, validates them, predicts one of three readiness categories with a normalized k-nearest-neighbours classifier, stores assessment history and produces a personalised report.

It is an academic prototype. It must not be used by an employer to make a hiring decision.

## Target Users

- College students preparing for placements
- Faculty mentors
- Placement-training coordinators

## High-Level Features

- Student preparation assessment
- ML-based readiness classification
- Personalised improvement recommendations
- CSV assessment history
- Downloadable text report
- Validation and controlled error handling
